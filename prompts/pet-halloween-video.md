# Pet Halloween Video Prompt

## Prompt

Create a playful short Halloween video from the supplied person-and-pet photo. Preserve the person's identity and the pet's breed, markings, size, and personality. Introduce a gentle magical glow, floating autumn leaves, and small sparkling particles. Give the pet a comfortable, non-restrictive Halloween accessory while the person receives a coordinated pumpkin-inspired costume transformation. Keep all movement friendly and believable, with stable paws, faces, fur detail, and anatomy. End with the person and pet looking toward the camera in a warm Halloween portrait. Vertical 9:16, bright cinematic lighting, charming and family-friendly.

## Negative prompt

Avoid changing the pet's species or markings, extra limbs, distorted paws, aggressive behavior, fear, restraints, unsafe costumes, face distortion, flickering, text, logos, or watermarks.
