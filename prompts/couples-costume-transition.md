# Couples Halloween Costume Transition Prompt

## Prompt

Create a short vertical Halloween costume-transition video using the supplied couple portrait. Preserve both people's identities, relative positions, facial features, and body proportions. Begin with their original clothing. A synchronized swirl of orange embers and soft magical smoke passes across the frame, transforming both subjects into coordinated Halloween costumes. Keep their expressions natural and affectionate, with smooth fabric motion, consistent hands, and realistic lighting. Use one continuous camera move and finish with both subjects posing together in a festive cinematic Halloween scene. Vertical 9:16, elegant rather than frightening, designed for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

## Negative prompt

Avoid face swapping, merged bodies, extra people, inconsistent costumes, duplicate limbs, unstable hands, flickering, harsh cuts, text, logos, watermarks, gore, or sexualized styling.
