# Pumpkin Head Transformation Prompt

## Prompt

Create a short cinematic Halloween transformation video from the supplied portrait photo. Preserve the subject's identity, pose, body proportions, and clothing. A warm orange glow gathers around the head as curling autumn leaves and tiny sparks move through the frame. The face smoothly transforms into a carved jack-o'-lantern with expressive glowing eyes and a playful grin. Keep the transformation clean and continuous, with stable anatomy, realistic lighting, subtle camera movement, and no sudden cuts. End on a confident hero pose in a moody Halloween setting. Vertical 9:16, social-media ready, polished cinematic look.

## Negative prompt

Avoid distorted faces, duplicate limbs, extra fingers, flickering, abrupt identity changes, unreadable text, watermarks, low resolution, excessive gore, or frightening injury details.
